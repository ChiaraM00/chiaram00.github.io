I am a PhD student in Combinatorics at the [University of Warwick](https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/sci/maths/), under the supervision of [Agelos Georgakopoulos](https://agelos.neocities.org/).

In a previous life, I worked as a systematic trading strategies developer at Goldman Sachs.
